# Operator positioning — Meridian AI (for the 20 marquee-India pitches)

Meridian AI is a boutique applied-AI studio. The microsite-builder may state
product/service claims ONLY from this file — never invent capabilities; respect
the forbidden claims. This is the operator for every Meridian → marquee pitch.

## One-liner
Meridian AI is a boutique applied-AI studio that embeds with your team and ships production-grade LLM and agent systems — on your data, with human-in-the-loop controls — in six weeks, not six quarters.

## Proof points
- technical: We ship on your existing stack — retrieval over your own data, function-calling agents wired into your current services, and evals plus guardrails in CI. No model training, no rip-and-replace, no new infrastructure to run.
- roi: Every engagement targets ONE measurable operating metric per quarter — deflected support tickets, faster underwriting, lower onboarding drop-off — and we don't scale the retainer until that metric moves.
- gtm: We've shipped copilots and agent workflows for regulated fintech, commerce, and SaaS teams; every deployment ships with an audit trail and a human-review path so risk and compliance can sign off.

## Tone rules
- Plain, senior, sentence case. Lead with the reader's problem or the metric, not the brand.
- Speak to the reader's role (a CFO hears margin and risk; a CTO hears architecture and control).
- No hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "game-changing").
- Name only real capabilities: applied LLM/agent builds, retrieval, evals, guardrails, human-in-the-loop.

## Forbidden claims
- Do not name specific Meridian clients, logos, or case-study numbers (they are not public).
- Do not promise fixed ROI percentages, revenue lift, or cost-savings figures.
- Do not claim Meridian builds, trains, or hosts foundation models, or is a licensed financial/data entity.
- Do not guarantee regulatory, PCI, RBI, or SOC 2 compliance for the client's use case.
- Do not claim 100% accuracy, zero hallucinations, or fully autonomous operation without human review.

fintech, payments, ecommerce, foodtech, SaaS, devtools, insurtech, mobility, gaming
